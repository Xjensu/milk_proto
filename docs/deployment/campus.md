# Campus and building profile

A campus MAY deploy one Anchor per floor or building zone and MAY pin an operator root for safety announcements. Floors without an anchor use the ordinary ad-hoc posture. Cross-floor delivery depends on human and device mobility unless fixed anchors supply contacts.

Floor attenuation, dwell time, anchor density, announcement rate, and delivery targets require a measured drill. No city-scale property follows from this profile. The current simulator does not provide calibrated clustered mobility evidence for such a claim.
