# Open Questions

Uncertainties carried forward between phases. Each entry: question → why it matters → what resolves it → current best guess + confidence.

**OQ-1 — Per-sender sequencing vs deletion/expiry.** SSB-style (feed, seq) reconciliation assumes gap-free logs; our TTL expiry creates gaps. → Affects sync protocol correctness (doc 06). → Resolve by designing reconciliation over message-ID sets (RBSR) rather than sequence ranges; verify no hidden dependency on density of seq numbers. Best guess: RBSR over IDs makes seq gaps harmless; seq numbers retained only for profile LWW. (M)

**OQ-2 — Real phone-to-phone GATT throughput across a mixed fleet.** Planning number 50–150 kbps is a conservative literature reading, not measured on a 2026 device matrix. → Drives contact-capacity model in doc 13. → Resolve with bench measurements (Phase 5) or newer published phone measurements. 2026-07 update: a 2025 engineering measurement (I14, forum-grade, phone↔MCU not phone↔phone) reports 250–680 kbps at 2M PHY + DLE, suggesting the 50–150 kbps band may be conservative on modern hardware; still unverified phone-to-phone — bench measurement remains required. (M)

**OQ-3 — iOS background↔background discovery behavior on current iOS.** Overflow-area behavior documented by reverse engineering circa 2020; Apple may have changed details. → Determines whether iOS nodes can relay at all in background. → Resolve by re-testing on current iOS or finding 2024+ published measurements. Best guess: still restricted; design treats iOS-background as poll-only leaf (safe under uncertainty). (M)

**OQ-4 — Routing composition performance under BLE-parameterized simulation.** Published DTN comparisons assume WiFi-class contact capacity and synthetic mobility. → Validates R-C1 tier composition and adaptive L. → Resolve with ONE-simulator (or custom) runs using Track B contact-capacity numbers at N=100/1k/10k (Phase 5, doc 13). 2026-07-22 update (E6-S1, `mmp-sim::tests_scale`): N=100/N=30 CI/stress scenarios reach delivery ratio 1.0 under `mmp-sim`'s uniform-random contact model; a first N=1,000 run under the same model reaches only 0.45, and only after compressing inter-contact gaps far below realistic cadence to fit the 72h TTL — meaning its p50/p90 latency output isn't in comparable real-world minutes and can't yet be checked against doc 13 §5's SLAW-band. Quota/store-growth cost claims (doc 13 §3) DO hold, measured, at N=1,000. Best guess updated: `mmp-sim` needs a clustered-mobility (SLAW or similar) contact model before the latency half of this question can be answered at all — uniform-random mixing alone measurably degrades with N and is not a stand-in for real same-district contact density. (M)

**OQ-5 — RBSR round-trip behavior over short BLE contacts.** Negentropy evidence is from Internet relays. → Sync efficiency during 5–30 s contacts (doc 06/13). → Resolve by modeling rounds × RTT against contact-duration distributions; prototype if needed. Best guess: Bloom pre-filter + ≤4 RBSR rounds fits typical contacts. (M)

**OQ-6 — RaptorQ IPR status.** Patents partially expired (~2024+), continuations exist, Qualcomm licensing commitment applies to RFC-compliant implementations. → Determines whether v2 can mandate RaptorQ. → Resolve with proper IPR review. v1 ships Reed-Solomon regardless. (L)

**OQ-7 — RS GF(256) decode cost on nRF52-class MCUs at k≤64.** Believed trivial (table-driven), not yet benchmarked by us. → IoT blob participation feasibility (doc 12/13). → Resolve with a `no_std` Rust benchmark in Phase 5. (M)

**OQ-8 — Group admin key rotation.** v1 fixes the admin set at group creation (doc 06 §8.4). → Long-lived groups need admin succession. → Resolve in v2 design: admin-set as its own signed CRDT with quorum rules; survey decentralized group-governance literature. (M)

**OQ-9 — Optional privacy profiles (relay-visible addressing).** Bundle headers expose origin/dest NodeIDs to carriers (doc 07 §5 note). → Largest deliberate metadata concession. → Resolve in v2: dest-only addressing with sealed origin, or per-conversation ephemeral dest IDs; quantify the routing-utility cost of each. (M)

**OQ-10 — External cryptographic review of non-standard compositions.** Serverless prekeys (08 §4.2), handshake-hash channel binding (08 §3.2, libp2p precedent), opportunistic DH ratchet (08 §5.2). → Custom compositions are where protocols die; internal review ≠ proof. → Resolve via external cryptographer review and/or formal modeling (Tamarin/ProVerif) before any production claim. (H importance, L current assurance)

**OQ-13 — Whole-phone energy multiplier (k_sys).** Radio-only arithmetic gives ~0.35%/day; SoC wakeups, foreground service, and FFI callbacks multiply it by an unmeasured k_sys ∈ [3,10] (doc 13 §1.2). → Decides whether <5%/day holds and which lever to pull first (burst cap → beacon interval → scan slot, in that order). → Resolve with on-device measurement (Android Battery Historian / iOS Instruments) on ≥3 device classes during Phase-5 implementation step 6. (E)

**OQ-12 — Bluetooth SIG 16-bit UUID assignment.** Development uses a 128-bit UUID; iOS background targeted scans and the 31-byte beacon budget both want the 16-bit Service Data form (doc 09 §2). → Production release blocker. → Resolve via SIG membership + assigned-numbers request (or validated fallback: 128-bit service data layout with 12 fewer payload bytes — quantify impact). (M)

**OQ-11 — Hybrid PQ Noise maturity for suite 0x02.** Hybrid X25519+ML-KEM-768 construction deliberately unpinned (08 §9). → Resolve when hybrid Noise patterns/PQ-Noise stabilize; track IETF and Noise-community drafts. (M)

**OQ-14 — Inter-carriage 2.4 GHz attenuation (train gangway).** No directly quotable measured dB figure obtained for carriage-to-carriage propagation loss at 2.4 GHz; two on-point papers exist but are paywalled (H11, H12). Converging indirect evidence — one-AP-per-carriage industry practice, dedicated 5.8/6.0 GHz directional inter-carriage bridge products (H8), and TGV WiFi bridging inter-car at 5 GHz not 2.4 GHz (H4) — indicates 2.4 GHz across carriage boundaries is unreliable. → Doc 17's train profile must decide whether BLE cells are per-carriage (anchors relay via passenger movement) or can span carriages. → Resolve via library access to H11/H12 or a field measurement. Best guess: treat each carriage as a separate BLE cell; inter-carriage delivery rides on passenger movement and the anchor backbone, not direct RF. (H importance, M confidence in the best guess)

**OQ-15 — Residual-contact-duration predictability on phones.** Can a phone estimate remaining contact time with a peer from RSSI slope or BLE 6.0 Channel Sounding (±1 m to 20 m measured on Pixel 10 via Android 16 RangingManager — I11/I13)? MANET link-lifetime prediction from RSSI exists (pre-BLE); no BLE work found. → Gates novelty N1 (contact-window-aware session admission, doc 17/15 R16). CS update rate unpublished (I3); iPhone CS support unknown. → Resolve via prototype measurement: RSSI-slope estimator quality on walking/vehicular traces. Best guess: coarse binary classification (long/short contact) is feasible from RSSI slope alone; fine-grained prediction needs CS. (H)

**OQ-16 — Operator authority-key provisioning path.** Profile A assumes QR pinning at boarding + claim gossip fallback (two trust levels). Unvalidated: QR-scan friction, poster-replacement attacks (fake QR over real one), and gossip-consistency detection quality. → Determines real-world trust floor of doc 18 announcements. → Resolve via UX study / abuse analysis in doc 18 personas review; field pilot later. Best guess: QR pinning acceptable for conductors/staff; passengers mostly rely on gossip consistency + trust banner. (M) *Round 2-bis annotation (CR2-2 soft spot):* every doc 18 §7 A6 containment except scope isolation presupposes the operator *notices* a root compromise, and no detection signal is specified — the natural candidate (gossip-consistency divergence on the root claim: two verifying-but-different roots for one scope observed by one node) extends this OQ's gossip-consistency question from provisioning to *compromise-detection latency*; fold into the same UX-study/pilot resolution path.

**OQ-17 — Revocation propagation latency vs authority-cert expiry.** Expiry-first revocation (doc 18) bounds abuse by cert lifetime; explicit revocation claims propagate at DTN speed (hours). V2X ACPC activation-code pattern (J9) is an alternative requiring periodic issuer connectivity. → Sets the conductor-key-compromise blast radius alongside the priority budget. → Resolve by modeling revocation-claim propagation vs typical cert lifetimes (shift-length) in the doc 17 train contact model. Best guess: shift-length certs (≤12 h) + budget clamp make explicit revocation a second-order defense. (M) *Round 2-bis annotation (CR2bis-2):* the object whose propagation this OQ models is now the **issuer-signed kill-cert** (doc 18 §6, sticky kill-wins), not the subject-signed tombstone — the tombstone is thief-defeatable and only covers retirement/loss; the modeling question is unchanged (claim-gossip speed vs shift-length expiry) but applies to the issuer's claim slot.

**OQ-18 — Thumbnail codec/size floor.** What is the minimum thumbnail byte size giving a useful preview (target ≤32 KiB ⚠, doc 16)? Depends on codec availability across no_std/mobile (JPEG baseline vs WebP vs raw downscale). → Sets the eager-dissemination cost per file share. → Resolve with a codec survey + visual-quality check during doc 16 drafting; implementation benchmark later. (L)
